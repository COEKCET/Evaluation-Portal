During Exam — foilcard & dummy entry

Pick a date and session, then key in QP code, foilcard range (alphanumeric, e.g. TAPR-260619 to TAPR-260625), dummy range and absentees. The tally turns green only when the dummy count matches the present count.

✏ Entry grid

Total Foilcard and Total Dummy are computed as you type (foilcard count uses the numeric tail, the alpha prefix must match). Present = Total students − Absentees. Duplicate dummy ranges are rejected on save.
📅Choose a date and session, then press Load courses.

Generate valuation bundles

Splits every course into bundles of the configured size, assigns a foilcard to each bundle, issues a Board Bundle No. (CSE-001, ECE-001 …) and works out the dummy range it carries — then verifies the last bundle against the dummy number you entered.

✅ Pre-flight check

The Generate button unlocks only after the examination is confirmed over.
Pick a start and end date to generate only that range (use the same date in both for a single day). Leave both blank for every date. Bundles on other dates are left untouched.

Revaluation — dummy number locator

After evaluation, type or upload the dummy numbers of the scripts applied for revaluation. Each one is traced to its Board Bundle No., Foilcard No., Course Code and Course Name, and a ready-to-print report can be downloaded.

Reports

Every form is produced in the exact layout of the office templates. Choose a board, or All to produce every board one after another in a single file.

🏆 Report logo

Upload the logo before downloading. It is placed at the top of every page of every report (PDF, Word and Excel) in place of the college name. PNG or JPG; a transparent PNG looks best. The logo is remembered in this browser.

📬 Central Valuation Circular — stage

Stage 1 – before allotment: courses and script counts come from the Data sheet, the external examiners come from the External Examiner list of that board, and the number of internal examiners is entered by hand below. Stage 2 – after the HoD has allotted faculty: choose stage 2 and generate again; the circular then lists the examiners actually allotted, internal and external.

🚗 TA chart — college-wise travelling allowance 0

TA varies with the examiner’s college. Each external examiner’s college is matched here (College Code first, then College Name, then City) and the amount is used in the Issue Register and every claim statement. A college that is not listed uses the default TA above. The amount is paid per day or per visit, as set in “TA is paid”.
📤Click or drop the TA chart here .xlsx / .xls / .csv — columns: College Code, College Name, College City, Distance (KM), TA Amount, Remarks
